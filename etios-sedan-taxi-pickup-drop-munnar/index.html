03 / Popular drops

Cochin, Alleppey and Varkala by sedan.

The routes our sedan guests ask for most, with planning notes for each.

Munnar to Cochin by Etios / sedan

Cochin is the most common sedan drop from Munnar: couples and small families heading to airport, hotel or onward connection.

Drive

About 130 km to the city and about 110 km to Cochin International Airport, typically 3.5 to 4.5 hours depending on traffic and rain.

Airport timing

Leave Munnar with a generous buffer before your flight. Share the flight time and we recommend a pickup time.

On the way

Cheeyappara and Valara Waterfalls are on the Kochi side of the highway. Extra stops change timing and fare, so ask in advance.

Munnar to Alleppey by Etios / sedan

A private sedan is a relaxed way for couples to reach Alleppey backwaters and houseboat stays.

Drive

About 165 km, usually 4.5 to 5.5 hours. If you are boarding a houseboat, plan around its boarding time.

Drop points

Jetty, resort, homestay or railway station; share the name and location.

Tip

Add a Kumarakom stop if you want a leisurely backwater day.

Munnar to Varkala by Etios / sedan

Varkala is a favourite for couples and small groups continuing from the hills to the cliff and beach.

Drive

Roughly 290 km, around 7 to 8.5 hours. Start early and plan meal and rest breaks.

Planning

Vehicle access near the cliff can be limited; share your stay name so we confirm the closest practical drop. A break in Alleppey or Kollam makes the day easier.

Onward

Trivandrum airport, Kovalam and Kollam drops can be arranged on request.
