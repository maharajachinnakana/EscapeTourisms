02 / The route

What this package covers.

This is the classic Munnar scenic route, packed into one early start. You leave your hotel in the dark, reach Top Station for first light, then work down through tea slopes, grassland, two lakes and a dam, and end with the tea factory and flower garden. Most travellers are back in town before the afternoon.

The fare is for the vehicle, so a family of six pays the same as a couple. If you want to drop a stop, add one or stretch the day, tell us on WhatsApp and we confirm the fare and timing before you travel.

Grassland views and Madupetty boating.

Photos taken on our Munnar sightseeing routes. Boating at Madupetty is a separate ticket, paid at the counter, and is not part of the vehicle fare.

Madupetty boating: what the rate board says

  • Boat rates (5-seat and 7-seat speed boats, 15 minutes) are listed in the Mattupetty stop above. Passenger limits include children.
  • A larger boat for up to 20 passengers runs 30 minutes; the price is not legible on our photo.
  • Boat rates are for the whole boat, not per person. Only one ticket is allowed per trip.
  • Entrance: adult ₹40, child (5 to 12) ₹20, student ₹20. Still camera ₹150, professional camera ₹600.
  • Parking for 3 hours: four-wheeler ₹40, van or tempo ₹60.

Read from the board at the Sun Moon Valley Boating Centre, Madupatty, when we photographed it. Kerala Hydel Tourism sets these rates and can change them, so treat them as a guide. Tickets are valid for the visit date only and are not refundable. A full Innova of 7 fits one 7-seat boat, about ₹200 each.
