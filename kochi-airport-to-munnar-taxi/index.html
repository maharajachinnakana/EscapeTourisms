01 / Quick answers

Cochin airport to Munnar, in short.

How far is Munnar from Cochin airport?

About 110 to 120 km by road, through Aluva, Perumbavoor, Kothamangalam and Adimali on the NH85 corridor.

How long does the drive take?

Plan around 3.5 to 4.5 hours without long stops; the last stretch is a hill road, so rain, fog and traffic can add time.

Is there a taxi waiting at the airport?

We arrange your pickup after you share your flight details, so the driver is ready when you land. Confirm the meeting point on WhatsApp before you fly.

What if my flight is late?

Share the flight number. The driver checks arrival time, so a delay does not mean a missed pickup.

How your Cochin airport pickup works.

Want a stop on the way?

Waterfall and tea-garden stops add time. If you must reach Munnar before dark, tell us so we keep stops short.

04 / Flight timing

Which flight times need extra planning.

Morning arrival

Easiest case. You reach Munnar by afternoon with daylight for stops.

Afternoon arrival

Plan a quick drive with one short stop. The hill section is better in daylight.

Late evening or night

The hill road is slower and darker. Many travellers stay near Cochin and start early, or choose a driver who knows the night route. Discuss this with us first.

Munnar to airport

Leave Munnar well before check-in. For international flights, we suggest leaving much earlier than you think; ask us for a start time.

06 / Good to know

Before you land.

Monsoon months

Heavy rain can slow the last 40 km. Keep a buffer, especially for connecting travel.

Hill-road comfort

If anyone feels motion sickness, ask for the front seat, a slower pace and a break after Adimali.

Phone and cash

Keep your phone active on arrival. Tolls and parking are stated in the quote.
