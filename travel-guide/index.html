Trip Planning

Munnar Travel Essentials

Best Time to Visit

September to March is the most pleasant season — cool weather and clear views. April–May is warmer; June–August is monsoon with lush greenery but more rain.

Weather & What to Pack

Munnar's hill climate stays cool year-round, dropping further at night. Carry a light jacket, comfortable shoes for sightseeing, and rain gear if visiting during monsoon.

How Many Days Do You Need?

2–3 days covers Munnar sightseeing and one jeep safari comfortably. Add more days if combining with Thekkady or Alleppey.

Kolukkumalai Timing

For sunrise at Kolukkumalai, jeeps leave Munnar around 3:30–4 AM. Book the evening before to confirm your slot.
