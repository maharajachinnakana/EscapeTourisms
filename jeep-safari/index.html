Route 1

Chaduranga Para Safari — What You'll See

Chaduranga Para View Point

Wide rock-top view point overlooking the valley — one of the best offroad viewpoints near Munnar.

Marachur Water Falls

A cascading forest waterfall reached via a short offroad stretch — a refreshing mid-route stop.

Spice Garden

Walk through a working spice garden — cardamom, pepper, coffee and more, with a guided walkthrough.

Anayirangal Dam

A calm reservoir stop framed by hills — popular for photos and a short break.

Lake View Point

Elevated stop with a wide view over the lake and surrounding tea estates.

Tea Garden View Point

Rolling tea plantation views to close out the route.

Route 2

Kolukkumalai Sunrise Safari — What You'll See

Jeep departs Munnar around 3:30–4:00 AM. The last stretch to Kolukkumalai is a steep offroad climb — only offroad jeeps make it, which is why timing matters for the sunrise.

Sunrise Point

One of South India's highest tea-estate view points — sunrise over the Western Ghats.

Lion Rock Point

A rock formation resembling a lion's face, with sweeping valley views nearby.

Tea Factory Visit

See how Kolukkumalai's high-altitude tea is hand-picked and processed at Asia's highest organic tea factory.
