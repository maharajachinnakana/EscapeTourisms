01 / Quick answers

Munnar jeep safari, in short.

How much is a jeep safari in Munnar?

Per jeep, up to 6 people: Kolukkumalai sunrise ₹3,200, Chaduranga Para ₹3,500 and a full-day Munnar jeep sightseeing ₹3,000.

Why a jeep and not a taxi?

Kolukkumalai’s last stretch is steep and rocky; only off-road jeeps can climb it. A regular taxi cannot.

Which safari should I pick?

Choose Kolukkumalai for a pre-dawn sunrise, Chaduranga Para for a relaxed daytime route with several stops.

Is the price per person?

No. It is per jeep, so six people share one fare.

02 / Choose a safari

Three jeep plans, three different days.

Every rate is per jeep for up to six travellers.

05 / Before you go

What to carry and how to prepare.

Warm layers

High viewpoints are windy and cold before sunrise, even in dry months.

Water and snacks

Facilities near the top are limited; carry your own.

Camera and power bank

Plan for photos; cold drains phone batteries faster.

Rain plan

Monsoon mist and wet tracks can change a safari; we advise on the day.
