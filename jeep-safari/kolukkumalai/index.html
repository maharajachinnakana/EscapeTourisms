What You'll See

The Route

Jeep departs Munnar around 3:30–4:00 AM. The last stretch to Kolukkumalai is a steep offroad climb — only offroad jeeps make it, which is why timing matters for the sunrise.

Sunrise Point

One of South India's highest tea-estate view points — sunrise over the Western Ghats.

Lion Rock Point

A rock formation resembling a lion's face, with sweeping valley views nearby.

Tea Factory Visit

See how Kolukkumalai's high-altitude tea is hand-picked and processed at one of the highest organic tea factories in the region.

Optional Add-On

Bose Peak Trekking

A guided trek near Kolukkumalai, bookable alongside the safari. ₹700+ per head, plus jeep waiting charge — let us know when booking so we can plan the timing.
