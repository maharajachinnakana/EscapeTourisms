01 / Quick answers

Kolukkumalai sunrise safari, in short.

What is Kolukkumalai?

A high-altitude tea estate at about 7,900 ft (2,400 m), widely described as one of the world’s highest tea estates.

How much is the safari?

₹3,200 per jeep for up to six people, covering Sunrise Point, Lion Rock Point and the tea factory visit.

When do we start?

Around 3:30–4:00 AM from Munnar so you reach the viewpoint before sunrise.

Can a car go up?

No. Only off-road jeeps manage the steep, rocky last stretch.

02 / The morning

How the sunrise morning unfolds.

Before 4 AM

Pickup from your Munnar, Chinnakanal or Suryanelli stay. Dress in layers.

The climb

A steep off-road track of roughly 32 km from Munnar, rough in places and best left to experienced jeep drivers.

Sunrise Point

Wait for first light over the Western Ghats. Cloud can hide the sun, so no sunrise is ever guaranteed.

Lion Rock Point

A rock outcrop that resembles a lion’s face, with big valley views nearby.

Tea factory

See orthodox tea made in a colonial-era factory with vintage machinery. Ask about the tea-making experience and packed tea on sale.

03 / Stops

What you stop at.

Sunrise Point

The main viewpoint. Expect cold wind and a quiet crowd.

Lion Rock Point

A natural rock face with sweeping views.

Tea factory

Orthodox processing, with the history of the estate on show.

04 / When to go

Best months and weather.

Seasons are general guides; the day’s sky decides your view.

Any monthCold before dawn; weather decides visibility
