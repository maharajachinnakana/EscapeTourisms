## Content standards for every page
1. Quick answers block (2-4 short Q&A) near the top: feeds AEO, voice search and AI answers.
2. One H1 with the primary query; each section answers one sub-question people ask.
3. FAQ = real "People also ask" style questions unique to this page (no copy between pages).
4. Facts: distances/times are rounded estimates; no fake prices, no guaranteed sightings.
5. Local/GEO: place names, route stages, hours of operation, Munnar/Devikulam/Chinnakanal mentions where true.
6. Schema: Service + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList + WebPage (speakable) + LocalBusiness.
