01 / Quick answers

Munnar taxi for international visitors.

Do drivers speak English?

Our drivers can help in English, Tamil, Malayalam and Hindi. Tell us your language when you book.

Are prices published?

Yes. Local sightseeing starts at ₹2,000 per day for a Swift Dzire, per vehicle, not per person.

Can you meet me at Cochin airport?

Yes. Cochin airport to Munnar is from ₹4,000 per vehicle (₹5,500 when a one-way taxi is not available).

How do I book from abroad?

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 94976 65450 with your flight details. Use the +91 country code.

03 / Arrival

From the airport to your hotel.

Before you fly

Send your flight number, arrival time, passengers and Munnar hotel on WhatsApp.

At Cochin airport

Your driver tracks the flight and we share the driver name and number.

The drive

Roughly 3.5 to 4.5 hours depending on traffic and stops. A tea or waterfall break is easy to add.

At Munnar

Your driver drops you at your hotel and can plan your sightseeing days with you.

04 / Before you book

Things you can ask us.

  • The exact fare per vehicle, in writing on WhatsApp
  • What is included and what is not (entry tickets and activities are separate)
  • Your driver’s name and number before pickup
  • Which languages your driver speaks
  • Pickup point for your flight, train or hotel
