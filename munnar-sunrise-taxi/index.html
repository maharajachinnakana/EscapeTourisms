03 / Choose Your Sunrise

Which Munnar sunrise point should you choose?

01

Top Station Sunrise

Mountain viewsTea-country sceneryRoad-accessiblePhotographyNo off-road safari

Top Station is one of the classic high-altitude viewpoints around Munnar. Kerala Tourism identifies Top Station at around 1,700 metres above sea level and describes it as a major viewpoint on the Munnar–Kodaikanal road. It is also associated with views towards the Western Ghats and Tamil Nadu.

The road to Top Station is winding and mountainous, so an early departure is important.

Starting taxi fare: ₹2,000 for Swift
02

Chokramudi Sunrise

Moderate trek3–4 hoursUphill walking

Chokramudi is a mountain trekking destination near Munnar. Kerala Tourism currently lists Chokramudi at about 18 km from Munnar, around 2,195 metres altitude, with a moderate trek of approximately 3–4 hours through tea plantations, shola forests and montane grasslands. Early mornings are particularly suitable for views.

This is not simply a drive-and-step-out sunrise point. Be prepared for uphill walking, uneven terrain, early-morning darkness, cooler temperatures, a multi-hour trek and weather-dependent access.

A current local trekking listing describes sunrise departures around 4:30 AM, although the exact meeting time depends on the operator and date.

The trek fee and access conditions should be reconfirmed before booking.
Taxi from ₹2,000 (Swift) + ₹500 Chokramudi trek
03

Kolukkumalai Sunrise

Off-road 4WD jeepUp to 6 per jeepTea factory visit possible

Kolukkumalai is one of the most distinctive sunrise experiences near Munnar. Kerala Tourism's Responsible Tourism programme describes it as a high-altitude tea plantation at approximately 7,900 feet, accessible by jeep or trekking, and specifically highlights its sunrise experience.

A normal taxi cannot complete the final Kolukkumalai route. The usual journey is Munnar → Suryanelli → 4WD Jeep → Kolukkumalai → Sunrise → Return. The final section is rugged off-road terrain, and current local operators commonly start the sunrise jeep around 4:00–4:30 AM from Suryanelli.

When booking, ask for: Munnar taxi + Kolukkumalai sunrise jeep safari — rather than treating the entire journey as a normal car trip.

The safari is separate from the Munnar taxi. Tea factory visit after sunrise is subject to current access and operating conditions.
Taxi from ₹2,000 (Swift) + ₹3,200 jeep safari per jeep
04

Chathurangapara Sunrise

Wide valley viewsWindmillsPhotographyOff-road experience

Chathurangapara is a high-wind viewpoint in Idukki with broad Western Ghats views. Kerala Tourism specifically recommends the viewpoint around sunrise or sunset, when the changing light creates the most dramatic views. It also warns visitors to maintain a safe distance from the cliff because wind speed can increase suddenly.

The safari/activity fee is separate from transportation.
Taxi from ₹2,400 (Swift) + ₹1,200 Chathurangapara jeep safari
05

Bodimettu Sunrise

Longer mountain driveAway from the busiest viewpoints

Bodimettu is a mountain route towards the Kerala–Tamil Nadu border and can be considered for travellers who want an early-morning hill journey away from the busiest Munnar viewpoints.

Because sunrise visibility depends heavily on cloud, mist and weather, the experience should be planned as a scenic sunrise attempt rather than a guaranteed sunrise view. Exact pickup time will be planned according to the travel date and expected sunrise.

Starting taxi fare: ₹3,000 for Swift

06 / Before You Go

Photography tips, weather & visibility.

Early-morning hill conditions can change quickly. Sunrise visibility depends on weather — no sunrise point can guarantee a clear sunrise on every day.

  • Carry a light jacket
  • Keep your phone/camera charged
  • Carry a small torch for trekking
  • Wear proper walking shoes for trekking routes
  • Carry water
  • Protect camera equipment from mist
  • Avoid standing close to cliff edges
  • Follow local guide/operator instructions

Weather & sunrise visibility

Munnar's hills can be covered by mist or cloud, particularly during wet weather. A sunrise trip can still be beautiful when clouds and mist are present, but the actual sun may not be visible.

Therefore, we do not promise a clear sky, visible sun, sea of clouds, specific colours or uninterrupted mountain views. The goal is to take you to the selected sunrise destination at the appropriate time and according to current access conditions.

07 / Important

Read before you book.

Kolukkumalai requires a jeep

If your destination is Kolukkumalai, do not book it as a normal car-only sunrise trip.

The practical route is: Munnar → Suryanelli by taxi → Kolukkumalai by local 4WD jeep → Sunrise → Return. The rough final section is unsuitable for ordinary cars, which is why the local jeep is part of the experience.

Chokramudi is a trek

Chokramudi is not simply a viewpoint beside the road. The Kerala Tourism destination information classifies it as a moderate guided trek lasting approximately 3–4 hours.

Wear suitable footwear and be prepared for an uphill trek. Weather conditions can affect access.

Chathurangapara has strong winds

Chathurangapara can experience high-speed winds. Kerala Tourism specifically advises visitors to stay away from cliff edges because wind intensity can change quickly.

Follow the driver's/local guide's safety instructions during the visit.
