Who We Are

Munnar-Based, Kerala-Wide

Escape Tourisms is a Munnar-based taxi and tourism company. We run Munnar sightseeing taxi, multi-day Kerala taxi packages, airport & station pickup-drop, and offroad jeep safari to Kolukkumalai and Chaduranga Para. Our drivers are local, know the roads and the weather, and can plan a route around your pace, not a fixed script.

Our fleet includes Etios sedans, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, offroad Jeeps, Swift Dzire and a Traveller Urbania for larger groups — covering everything from a solo sightseeing day to a full family Kerala tour.
