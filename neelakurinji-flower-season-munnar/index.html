Neelakurinji Flower Season, Munnar

A rare Neelakurinji bloom is happening around Munnar's Chokramudi and Mattupetty hills right now in 2026 — a full 4 years ahead of the famous Eravikulam bloom. Book a guided trek with transport included.

Good To Know

Why Is Neelakurinji Blooming in 2026?

Munnar actually has more than one Neelakurinji population, each on its own 12-year cycle. The population inside Eravikulam National Park bloomed in 2018 and is next expected in 2030. A separate population around Chokramudi and the Mattupetty-Korandakkad hills last bloomed in 2014 — making 2026 its year. This is the bloom currently drawing visitors to Munnar right now.

Where To See It

3 Neelakurinji Trekking Spots Near Munnar

Each spot has a different character and access process — our guide will help you choose based on your fitness level and time.

1. Chokramudi Hills

The main, most active bloom site this season, near Gap Road. Purely a trekking activity across rocky, high-altitude terrain.

Local entry charge: approx. ₹200 per head

2. Korandakkad, near High Range School (Mattupetty)

A roughly 2 km trek near the High Range School in Korandakkad, on hillside owned by a private tea company.

Entry requires permission from the plantation company — our guide handles this on the day.

3. Chinnakanal Vilakku (60 Acre)

A quieter, private 60-acre spot known mostly to locals — far fewer visitors than Chokramudi. Since it's not a marked tourist spot, a local guide is strongly recommended.

No entry charge currently.
