01 / Quick answers

Munnar to Alleppey, in short.

How far is Alleppey from Munnar?

About 170 km by the main road through Adimali, Kothamangalam, Muvattupuzha and Changanassery.

How long does the drive take?

Around 5 hours with a couple of short breaks, longer on weekends or in heavy rain.

Can I reach a houseboat on time?

Yes if you plan the start. Houseboat check-in is fixed by the operator, so tell us your boarding time and we work backwards.

Is it a good one-day trip?

It is a long transfer day. Most travellers treat it as a move from hills to backwaters, not a round trip.

02 / Two ways down

Pick the route that matches your plan.

Main route: NH85 / NH183

Munnar, Adimali, Kothamangalam, Muvattupuzha, Changanassery, Alleppey. About 170 km, the faster option with ghat curves only at the start.

Scenic route via Thekkady

Munnar, Thekkady, Kottayam, Alleppey. Longer, but passes spice country and works if you also want Periyar.

03 / Houseboat day

Planning around a houseboat check-in.

Houseboat boarding times are set by the operator, often around midday, so ask them for your exact slot.

Work backwards

Take the boarding time, subtract about five hours of driving, then add a buffer for breaks and traffic.

Lunch

If you arrive before check-in, plan a meal near Alleppey rather than waiting at the jetty.

Luggage

Houseboats have limited storage. Pack light for the cruise and keep big bags in the car if the operator allows.

05 / Choosing

Alleppey or Kumarakom?

Alleppey

Busy, canal-heavy and the classic houseboat base. Better for a short stay with plenty to do.

Kumarakom

Quieter backwater resorts, a little farther inland. Ask us to adjust the drop.
