01 / In short

How our Munnar tour packages work.

Transport-led

We provide the vehicle, driver and route. Hotel, entry tickets and activities are booked separately, so you only pay for what you choose.

Priced per vehicle

The fare does not change with the number of travellers, so a full car costs less per person.

Flexible

Swap, add or drop stops before you travel. Tell us what you like and we adjust the day.

Foreign guests welcome

Fares are shown in rupees and drivers can help in English. See Munnar taxi for foreign tourists.

02 / Day packages

Six one-day Munnar packages.

Each page lists every stop, the entry tickets and the fare for every vehicle.

Early Morning Scenic Tour

A full scenic day that starts before sunrise at Top Station and ends with flowers, tea and Mattupetty. One private vehicle, one local driver, eleven stops, all finished before the afternoon.

from ₹2,300
Innova ₹3,000

Kolukkumalai & Lockhart Tea Estate

Climb Kolukkumalai in a jeep for sunrise, then spend the day on the tea-estate side of Munnar: Anayirangal lake, Periyakanal falls, Lockhart tea garden and the botanical garden.

from ₹2,300
Innova ₹3,000

Adventure Tour

Hot-air balloon, glass bridge, a 1.8 km zipline and an adventure park, with a spice garden, chocolate factory and two waterfalls to cool off in between.

from ₹3,000
Innova ₹3,700

Chokramudi Trek & Lockhart

A sunrise trek at Chokramudi, then an easy second half through the Lockhart tea country, Idli Hills and the botanical garden.

from ₹2,000
Innova ₹2,700

Chathurangapara Sunrise

The longest package: a Chathurangapara jeep sunrise, a waterfall bath, a spice garden and then the whole Lockhart and Anayirangal circuit.

from ₹3,200
Innova ₹4,000

Eravikulam, Tea Museum & Lakkam

A slower, family-friendly day: the tea museum, Eravikulam National Park, the Kannan Devan tea hills and a bath at Lakkam waterfall.

from ₹2,000
Innova ₹3,000
