01 / Quick answer

The nearest airport to Munnar is Cochin.

Cochin International Airport (COK) at Nedumbassery is about 110 to 120 km from Munnar. The drive takes 3.5 to 4.5 hours because the last stretch climbs through the hills.

Nearest by road

03 / Which to pick

Choosing the right airport.

Flying in from abroad

Choose Cochin. It has the most international connections and the shortest drive. Read the taxi guide for foreign tourists.

Coming from Chennai or Bangalore

Compare Coimbatore and Madurai with Cochin. The drive from Coimbatore goes via Pollachi and Marayoor and is scenic, though it is not always shorter in time.

Combining the coast

Trivandrum suits trips that add Kovalam or Varkala, but plan a long day or a night halt. See Trivandrum and Kovalam.

04 / By train

Is there a railway station in Munnar?

No. Travellers use Aluva or Ernakulam stations and continue to Munnar by taxi, which is roughly 130 km.

We arrange pickups from the station. Share your train number and arrival time. See Aluva and Ernakulam station to Munnar taxi.

05 / Arrival tips

Before you land.

Aim for daylight

The hill bends are easier before dusk, so a morning flight works best.

Tell us the luggage

Five to seven travellers with suitcases need an Innova. See Innova pickup and drop.

Add a break

Waterfall and tea-stall stops are popular on the way. Tell the driver if you want one.

Plan your first day

Many guests start with sunrise the next morning. See the Munnar sightseeing packages.
