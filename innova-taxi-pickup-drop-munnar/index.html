Do you provide Innova pickup and drop from Munnar?

03 / Popular drops

Cochin, Alleppey and Varkala by Innova.

The routes our Innova guests ask for most, with practical planning notes for each.

Munnar to Cochin by Innova

Cochin is the busiest Innova route from Munnar, used for airport transfers, onward stays in Ernakulam and Fort Kochi, and connections to the rest of Kerala.

Drive

Roughly 130 km to the city and about 110 km to Cochin International Airport. Allow around 3.5 to 4.5 hours depending on traffic, rain and the exact drop point.

On the way

Cheeyappara and Valara Waterfalls are on the Kochi side of the highway, and tea-garden and viewpoint stops can be added. Extra stops change the timing and fare, so mention them when you request.

Best for

Families and groups of 5 to 7 with luggage, airport drops, and Kochi hotel drops where everyone travels together.

Munnar to Alleppey by Innova

Alleppey is the most requested backwater drop. An Innova suits families and groups heading to houseboats, beach hotels and homestays.

Drive

Roughly 165 km, typically 4.5 to 5.5 hours. Start early if you have a houseboat boarding time, since boarding slots are fixed.

Drop points

Houseboat jetty, resort, homestay or railway station. Share the exact name and location so the drop point is clear.

Tip

Combine Munnar to Alleppey with a Kumarakom or Kottayam stop if you want a slower day.

Munnar to Varkala by Innova

Varkala is a long-distance Innova trip, popular with families and friends continuing from the hills to the beach.

Drive

Roughly 290 km. Expect a full travel day of about 7 to 8.5 hours. Early departure is recommended.

Planning

If your plan is flexible, break the day with an Alleppey or Kollam stop. Share your property name; vehicle access near the cliff area can be limited, and we confirm the closest practical drop.

Also from Varkala

Onward drops to Trivandrum airport, Kovalam and Kollam can be arranged on request.
