02 / Choose Your Sunset

Which sunset point should you choose?

01

Pothamedu View Point

CouplesFamiliesPhotographyTea plantation viewsShort evening tripsNear Munnar town

Pothamedu is one of the easiest choices for a short Munnar sunset trip. Kerala Tourism describes Pothamedu as a viewpoint around 6 km from Munnar town, with broad views over tea, coffee and cardamom plantations. Kerala Tourism specifically highlights the sunset here as a scenic experience.

Starting taxi fare: ₹500 for Swift
02

Idly Hills Sunset

Short scenic drive

Idly Hills is suitable for travellers looking for a short scenic evening drive. The location works well when you do not want to spend several hours travelling to a distant sunset destination. It can be planned as a simple Hotel → Idly Hills → Sunset/photo stop → Hotel.

The exact visibility from the viewpoint depends on clouds, mist, terrain and the position of the sun on the travel date.

Starting taxi fare: ₹500 for Swift
03

2nd Mile Tea Garden Sunset

Tea plantation photographyCouplesFamiliesShort evening trips

2nd Mile can work well for travellers who want a shorter evening outing around the Munnar tea country. The tea-covered hills become particularly photogenic as the afternoon light changes. This is a better choice for a relaxed local evening than for travellers specifically looking for a long-distance mountain sunset expedition.

Starting taxi fare: ₹800 for Swift
04

Door of Heaven Sunset

CouplesPhotographyScenic drivesEvening viewpoints

Door of Heaven can be considered for travellers looking for a dramatic hill-and-valley viewpoint during the evening. The experience is primarily about the landscape, changing light and photography rather than a formal attraction with a guaranteed sunset.

Weather and visibility can change the experience considerably.

Starting taxi fare: ₹1,500 for Swift
05

Gap Road Sunset

Road-trip photographyMountain viewsCouplesScenic drive

Gap Road is known for its dramatic mountain-road scenery and valley views. Rather than treating it as a single fixed attraction, the sunset experience here is about the surrounding landscape, road viewpoints and changing light across the hills.

The safest stops depend on the exact route, traffic and available roadside stopping areas. Do not stop in unsafe road sections simply for photographs.

Starting taxi fare: ₹1,700 for Swift
06

Top Station Sunset

Mountain sceneryPhotographyLong scenic drivesCouples

Top Station is a high-altitude viewpoint on the Munnar–Kodaikanal road. Kerala Tourism identifies it as one of Munnar's major panoramic viewpoints, with views across the surrounding mountains, valleys and tea plantations.

A sunset trip to Top Station is better suited to travellers who want a longer mountain drive rather than a quick evening viewpoint. Because the return journey is through winding mountain roads, allow sufficient time after sunset rather than planning an extremely late return.

Starting taxi fare: ₹2,000 for Swift
07

Kolukkumalai Sunset

Off-road 4WD jeepAdventure

Kolukkumalai is the most adventurous option on this list. The final journey from Suryanelli to Kolukkumalai uses a rugged off-road route that requires a local 4WD jeep. Current local safari information describes the Suryanelli → Kolukkumalai route as approximately 13 km, with a substantial off-road section.

A sunset trip generally starts much later than a sunrise trip. Current local safari operators advertise afternoon departures around 3:00 PM for sunset visits, but the exact departure should be confirmed according to the travel date and operator.

The Escape Tourisms Kolukkumalai safari page lists the sunrise safari at ₹3,200 per jeep. For a sunset booking, confirm the applicable current safari rate and timing before travel.

03 / Kolukkumalai Sunset

Munnar sunset taxi + Kolukkumalai jeep.

If you want Kolukkumalai sunset, book it as two parts. This is important because an ordinary taxi cannot simply drive all the way to the top.

1. Munnar Taxi

Transport from your hotel to the Suryanelli/base point.

2. Kolukkumalai Jeep Safari

A local 4WD jeep takes you through the off-road section to Kolukkumalai.

The practical route

MunnarSuryanelliKolukkumalai 4WD JeepSunsetReturn

The local jeep safari is an additional activity, separate from the Munnar taxi. The final route is rough, steep and weather-dependent.

Kolukkumalai is not a normal taxi route

A normal taxi should not be treated as the vehicle for the final Kolukkumalai off-road climb. The standard practical arrangement is Munnar → Suryanelli by taxi → Kolukkumalai by 4WD jeep. Book the taxi and jeep as separate components of the experience.

04 / Timing & Planning

What time should we leave for sunset?

There is no single fixed sunset time throughout the year.

The correct departure time depends on

  • 01Travel date
  • 02Selected sunset point
  • 03Hotel location
  • 04Road distance
  • 05Traffic
  • 06Weather
  • 07Whether an off-road jeep is required

For a nearby viewpoint such as Pothamedu, the departure can be much later than for Kolukkumalai. For Top Station or other longer routes, allow enough time for the mountain drive and return. For Kolukkumalai, the local jeep operator determines the practical reporting and departure time. We will plan the pickup around the actual sunset time on your travel date rather than using one fixed daily time.

How long does a sunset trip take?

It depends on the destination. A nearby viewpoint can be a short evening trip, while Top Station or Kolukkumalai requires substantially more travel time. Your itinerary can be planned as:

Short sunset trip

Hotel → Nearby viewpoint → Sunset → Hotel

Scenic evening trip

Hotel → Viewpoint → Sunset → Optional food stop → Hotel

Adventure sunset

Hotel → Suryanelli → Kolukkumalai Jeep → Sunset → Return

Do not choose a distant sunset point if you have a strict evening schedule.

Sunset taxi from your Munnar hotel

Pickup can be arranged from your Munnar hotel, resort, homestay, Munnar town or another agreed pickup location. Share your stay location when booking so the pickup time can be planned correctly.

Can I visit more than one sunset point?

Yes, but it is usually better to choose one main sunset location. Trying to visit multiple distant viewpoints immediately before sunset can result in spending the evening inside the vehicle instead of enjoying the view.

A practical combination can be Pothamedu → 2nd Mile → final sunset stop, when road conditions and timing allow. The final stop should be selected based on the actual sunset time.

06 / Before You Go

Sunset photography, weather & visibility.

Sunset photography in Munnar depends heavily on weather. Clouds can sometimes make the sky more dramatic, but they can also hide the sun. No sunset trip can guarantee a visible sun or a particular sky colour.

  • Reach the viewpoint before sunset
  • Carry a charged phone/camera
  • Keep a light jacket
  • Protect equipment from mist
  • Use a stable position for low-light photographs
  • Avoid standing near unsafe cliff edges
  • Follow local instructions

Weather & visibility

Munnar's mountain weather can change quickly. Mist, cloud, rain and low visibility can affect mountain views, valley views, sunset visibility, photography and road conditions.

The taxi service can take you to the selected destination, but natural conditions cannot be controlled. If weather makes a particular route unsafe, the itinerary may need to be adjusted.
