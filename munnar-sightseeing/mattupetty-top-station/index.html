03 / On The Way

Places to see on the way to Top Station.

01

Flower Garden

A convenient early stop for travellers who enjoy flowers, greenery and a slower start to the sightseeing day. Flower Garden is also known locally as a rose garden and is associated with tea-growing landscapes around Munnar. Entry and operating details can change, so confirm current information before your visit.

02

Tea Garden & Photo Point

Stop among Munnar's tea-covered hills for photographs and mountain views. Photo Point is one of the scenic stops close to Munnar and fits naturally into the route toward Mattupetty and Top Station.

03

Horse Riding

Optional activity

Horse riding may be available at selected points along the route. This is an optional paid activity and is not part of the taxi fare. Availability and activity charges should be confirmed locally on the day.

04

Carmelagiri Elephant Park

Optional activity

Carmelagiri Elephant Park is located on the road toward Mattupetty. Travellers can choose to include the park as an optional activity stop. Elephant activities are separate from your taxi fare, and current operating conditions and charges should be confirmed before participating.

05

Mattupetty Tea Factory

A tea-factory visit can add a tea-production experience to the route. Access to individual factories can depend on their visitor arrangements and operating schedule. We recommend confirming access before making it a fixed part of your itinerary.

06

Mattupetty Dam & Lake

Mattupetty is one of the main stops on the Top Station route. The dam and lake are surrounded by tea plantations, forest and mountain scenery. Boating is available as a separate activity when operating.

07

Sun Moon Valley Boating

Optional activity

If you want to add boating to your sightseeing day, the Mattupetty area offers boating options. Boat type, availability and activity charges are separate from your taxi fare.

08

Wild Elephant Sightings

The Mattupetty landscape is known for occasional wildlife sightings, including elephants. However, wild elephant sightings are never guaranteed. Wildlife movement is unpredictable and travellers should never approach or disturb wild animals.

09

Echo Point

Echo Point is one of the popular stops between Mattupetty and the higher Top Station route. It is known for its echo effect, lake setting and surrounding hills. Entry or activity charges, if applicable, are separate from the taxi fare and may change.

10

Kundala Lake

Kundala Lake is another scenic stop on the Top Station route, surrounded by hills, forest and tea plantations. Boating may be available as an optional activity.

11

Yellappetty

Yellappetty is associated with the eastern mountain landscape toward the Vattavada side. The area is known for agricultural landscapes, tea-growing hills and views toward the Kerala–Tamil Nadu mountain region. If you want to continue toward Vattavada, an additional route charge applies.

12

Top Station

Main destination

Top Station is the main destination of this route and is known for its elevated viewpoint over the Western Ghats and the Tamil Nadu side. It is also associated with the seasonal Neelakurinji landscape. Neelakurinji is seasonal and should not be presented as a year-round attraction.

04 / Time & Photos

How long does it take?

The direct road distance between Munnar and Top Station is approximately 34 km, but a sightseeing trip takes much longer than a direct drive because of the attractions and stops along the route.

Your total trip duration depends on

  • 01Number of stops
  • 02Time spent at each attraction
  • 03Boating or other activities
  • 04Traffic
  • 05Weather
  • 06Road conditions
  • 07Photography stops
  • 08Lunch breaks

For this reason, a sightseeing taxi should be planned as a day route, rather than as a simple point-to-point transfer.

Can you stop for photos?

Yes. The Top Station route has many scenic viewpoints and tea-covered mountain landscapes. Reasonable photography stops can be included in your itinerary. If you want to spend longer at specific locations, tell us before the trip so the route can be planned accordingly.

Hotel & resort pickup

You can request pickup from your Munnar hotel or resort. Provide the exact accommodation location while booking so we can confirm the pickup point and any additional distance requirement.

06 / Good To Know

Before you go.

Is a jeep required?

A normal taxi can travel to Top Station under normal road conditions. A separate off-road Jeep is not automatically required for the regular Top Station road. A Jeep becomes relevant when you choose an off-road activity or a route that specifically requires one.

Neelakurinji

Neelakurinji flowering is seasonal. Top Station can be visited throughout the normal tourism season, but travellers should not expect Neelakurinji flowers on every visit.

If your trip is specifically planned around Neelakurinji flowering, confirm the current seasonal status before booking.

Are tickets included?

No. Attraction tickets, boating, horse riding, elephant activities, trekking, Jeep safaris and other paid experiences are separate unless explicitly included in your booking.

Food stops

Reasonable food stops can be included depending on the route and available time. Food and drinks are at your own expense.

What happens if it rains?

Munnar's mountain weather can change quickly. Rain, mist or reduced visibility may affect viewpoints, outdoor activities and the time required for the route. If weather conditions make a particular stop unsuitable, the itinerary may need to be adjusted.

Can I see wild elephants?

You may encounter wildlife in the Mattupetty area, but wild elephant sightings cannot be guaranteed. Never approach, feed, follow or disturb wild animals.

07 / Who It's For

Families & couples.

Is this route suitable for families?

Yes. Families can choose the vehicle according to the number of travellers and plan the route with reasonable breaks. If you are travelling with young children or elderly passengers, tell us in advance so we can avoid an unnecessarily rushed itinerary.

Is this route good for couples?

Yes. The Top Station route works well for couples who want tea gardens, mountain viewpoints, lake stops and photography without joining a shared sightseeing vehicle. You can also combine this route with a sunrise-focused trip if your travel plan requires an early departure. For a dedicated sunrise itinerary, see our Munnar Sunrise Taxi service.
