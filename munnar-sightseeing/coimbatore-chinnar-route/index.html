02 / The Route

Munnar to Coimbatore route.

Munnar to Coimbatore is a cross-state road journey from the hills of Kerala into Tamil Nadu. The exact road distance varies according to the route and final destination within Coimbatore, so it is better not to plan the journey around one fixed kilometre figure.

The direct journey is substantially shorter than a sightseeing itinerary. Once you add attractions such as the Tea Museum, Eravikulam National Park or Lakkam Waterfall, the total travel time depends on the number of sightseeing stops, time spent at each attraction, traffic, weather, road conditions, attraction opening hours and your required Coimbatore arrival time.

If you have a flight, train, bus or other fixed appointment in Coimbatore, tell us the required arrival time before adding sightseeing stops.

03 / Sightseeing

Sightseeing places on the Munnar route.

01

Tea Museum

FamiliesCouplesTea loversPhotography

The Kannan Devan Tea Museum is one of the most relevant stops for travellers interested in Munnar's tea history. The museum presents the history of tea plantations in Munnar and displays old machinery, photographs and equipment connected with tea production. Visitors can also learn about tea processing and tasting.

The museum operates separately from the taxi service, so the museum ticket is not included in the taxi fare. Published information lists museum visiting hours during the daytime, but timings can change. Confirm the current opening schedule before planning the stop.

Entry: separate.
02

Eravikulam National Park

Nature loversWildlifeMountain landscapesPhotography

Eravikulam National Park is one of Munnar's major wildlife and mountain attractions. The park is known for the endangered Nilgiri Tahr and its high-altitude grassland and shola landscape. Entry is controlled by the park authorities and the visit follows the current park access system.

The official park information currently lists entry time 8:00 AM–2:00 PM, Indian adult entry ₹200, foreign national entry ₹500, ordinary camera ₹50 and video camera ₹350. The park also has a seasonal closure period — the 2026 official closure was from 1 February to 31 March. Because park timings, closure periods and ticket structures can change, confirm the current status before your travel date. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.

Entry: separate.
03

Kannan Devan Tea Hills View Point

Tea plantation viewsPhotographyShort scenic stop

The Kannan Devan Hills landscape is one of the defining features of Munnar. Tea plantations spread across the hills and valleys around Munnar, making selected viewpoints useful for travellers who want photographs without spending several hours inside a major attraction. This is primarily a scenic viewpoint experience, not a ticketed tea-factory visit. The exact stopping point can depend on the road and safe parking conditions.

Entry: depends on the specific viewpoint/location.
04

Lakkam Waterfall

WaterfallsNaturePhotographyShort walks

Lakkam Waterfall is located on the Munnar–Marayoor side and is associated with the Eravikulam landscape. It is a more nature-focused stop than a simple roadside viewpoint. Water flow can change considerably according to rainfall and season, so the experience is not identical throughout the year. The Kerala Forest Department's tourism material lists Lakkam Waterfalls among the attractions associated with Eravikulam National Park and separately identifies a visit charge. Because local access and fees can change, confirm the current entry conditions before the trip.

Entry: separate.

06 / Flexibility

Can I customize the route?

Yes. You can choose which attractions you want to include. You can:

  • Visit only the Tea Museum
  • Add Eravikulam National Park
  • Add a tea plantation viewpoint
  • Add Lakkam Waterfall
  • Remove attractions you are not interested in
  • Spend more time at one attraction
  • Keep the sightseeing short
  • Travel directly to Coimbatore after selected stops

The final route should be planned around attraction timings and your required arrival time.

How much time should I keep?

A direct Munnar → Coimbatore journey and a sightseeing journey are two different plans. A direct transfer can be completed much faster than a day involving multiple attraction visits.

For sightseeing, allow additional time for parking, entry procedures, walking, attraction queues, photography, activity duration, traffic, weather and road conditions.

If you need to reach Coimbatore at a specific time, tell us that time first. We can then suggest a practical number of stops rather than overloading the itinerary.

Eravikulam National Park timing & closure

If Eravikulam National Park is part of your itinerary, check its current operating status before the trip. The park has a seasonal closure period, and the exact dates can change according to the official management decision.

Do not book the route assuming the park will automatically be open on every date. If the park is closed, the sightseeing plan can be adjusted around other Munnar attractions.

Weather & road conditions

Munnar's hill roads can experience rain, mist and reduced visibility. Lakkam Waterfall can also change considerably according to rainfall. For safety and practical travel planning, the route may need to be adjusted when weather or road conditions affect a particular stop. Scenic views and waterfall conditions cannot be guaranteed.
