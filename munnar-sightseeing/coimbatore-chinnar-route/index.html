Points on This Route (Extra)

These stops are additional to the base route. Some involve trekking or wildlife sanctuary access — confirm current access conditions with our team.

Tea Museum

A museum tracing the history of tea cultivation in the region.

Extra — additional charge applies

Eravikulam National Park

A protected high-altitude national park, home to the Nilgiri tahr.

Entry tickets extra, subject to park timings

Lakkam Waterfalls

A forest waterfall and the base-fare destination for this route.

Route destination — included in base fare

Sandalwood Forest

A forest stretch known for sandalwood trees.

Extra — additional charge applies

Thoovanam Waterfalls Trek

A trek leading to a waterfall inside Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary.

Trekking required, booked separately

Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary

A protected sanctuary on the Tamil Nadu border, known for its dry deciduous forest.

Access subject to forest department rules
