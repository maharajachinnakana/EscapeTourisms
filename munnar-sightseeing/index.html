03 / What You'll See

Places covered in Munnar sightseeing.

01

Mattupetty Dam

A scenic reservoir and one of the popular stops on the classic Munnar sightseeing route, surrounded by tea estates and hills. Boating is available as a separate activity when operating.

02

Echo Point

A lakeside sightseeing stop surrounded by hills and forest, known for its echo effect. Commonly combined with Mattupetty, Kundala and Top Station — a short, easy stop.

03

Kundala Lake

A peaceful mountain lake on the route toward Top Station, surrounded by tea plantations and hills.

04

Top Station

One of the major viewpoints around Munnar, on the Munnar–Kodaikanal road, with views toward the Tamil Nadu side of the Western Ghats — best in clear weather. Also associated with the seasonal Neelakurinji landscape.

05

Eravikulam National Park

Known for its high-altitude grasslands and the endangered Nilgiri Tahr. Entry tickets and the park's internal transport are paid separately and are not part of the taxi fare. The park has a seasonal closure period, so confirm its status for your date.

06

Tea Garden Viewpoints

Stop along suitable viewpoints to experience Munnar's tea-covered hills — rolling green plantations for photos and a signature Munnar stop.

07

Attukad Waterfalls

Located on the Munnar–Pallivasal side, Attukad offers a waterfall experience with trails and scenic surroundings. Waterfall flow changes with rainfall and season.

04 / Plan Your Day

A typical Munnar sightseeing day.

This is our most-requested route. A sightseeing day can begin around 8 AM; tell us your interests when booking and we'll re-order stops to fit your pace. The exact route does not have to follow this sample.

  1. 8:00 AM — Pickup from Munnar town / your hotelDriver arrives on time, with a quick briefing on the day's route.
  2. 9:00 AM — Mattupetty Dam & Echo PointOptional boating at Mattupetty, then a short stop at Echo Point.
  3. 11:00 AM — Top StationValley viewpoint on the Kerala–Tamil Nadu side of the Western Ghats.
  4. 1:00 PM — Lunch breakStop at a local restaurant along the route (own expense).
  5. 2:30 PM — Eravikulam National ParkEntry tickets at the counter; the park's shuttle takes you in — subject to the park's operating schedule.
  6. 4:00 PM — Tea Garden Viewpoint & Attukad WaterfallsPhoto stops through the tea estates on the way back.
  7. 6:00 PM — Drop back at Munnar town / hotelThe day ends back where it started.

How many places can you visit in one day?

A full-day trip typically covers several spots — around 6 to 8 including Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point, Top Station and tea garden viewpoints — but the number depends on the route, road conditions, time spent at each location and the activities you choose.

Trying to combine attractions from completely different directions increases driving time. For a better experience, choose attractions that naturally fit together on the same route.
