What You'll See

Places Covered in Munnar Sightseeing

Mattupetty Dam

Scenic reservoir surrounded by tea estates and hills — boating available, one of the most-visited Munnar view points.

Echo Point

A quiet lakeside stop known for its natural echo effect, surrounded by forested hills — a short, easy stop.

Top Station

The highest point on the Munnar–Kodaikanal road with panoramic valley views into Tamil Nadu — best in clear morning weather.

Eravikulam National Park

Home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr and rolling grasslands — entry tickets and shuttle are paid separately at the gate.

Tea Garden View Point

Rolling green tea plantations for photos and a walk through the estate rows — a signature Munnar stop.

Attukad Waterfalls

A short trail leads to a two-tier waterfall through forest — a refreshing stop on the sightseeing route.

A Typical Munnar Sightseeing Day

This is our most-requested route. Tell us your interests when booking and we'll re-order stops to fit your pace.

  1. 8:00 AM

    Pickup from Munnar town / your hotel

    Driver arrives on time, quick briefing on the day's route.

  2. 9:00 AM

    Mattupetty Dam & Echo Point

    Optional boating at Mattupetty, then a short stop at Echo Point.

  3. 11:00 AM

    Top Station

    Valley view point on the Kerala–Tamil Nadu border.

  4. 1:00 PM

    Lunch break

    Stop at a local restaurant along the route (own expense).

  5. 2:30 PM

    Eravikulam National Park

    Entry tickets at the counter; shuttle takes you into the park.

  6. 4:00 PM

    Tea Garden View Point & Attukad Waterfalls

    Photo stops through the tea estates on the way back.

  7. 6:00 PM

    Drop back at Munnar town / hotel

    Day ends back where it started.
