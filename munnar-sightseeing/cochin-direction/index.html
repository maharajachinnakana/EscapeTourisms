02 / The Route

Munnar to Kochi route.

The usual road journey from Munnar towards Kochi passes through the hill roads towards Adimali, Neriamangalam and the Kochi side.

Usual road journey

MunnarAdimaliNeriamangalamKochi

The direct road journey is commonly quoted at roughly 125–130 km, with around 4 hours of normal driving time. Actual travel time can change because of traffic, rain, road conditions, hotel pickup location and sightseeing stops. For a sightseeing trip, it is better to plan the day around the places you actually want to visit rather than treating the route as a fixed four-hour transfer.

03 / Sightseeing

Sightseeing places on the Kochi direction.

01

Hydel Park

FamiliesRelaxed walksPhotographyShort stop

Hydel Park, also known as Blossom Hydel Park, is close to Munnar town on the Kochi direction. It is suited to a short nature and family stop, with landscaped areas, greenery and views around the Muthirapuzha River and surrounding tea hills. Kerala Hydel Tourism also lists facilities and activities associated with the park.

02

Pothamedu View Point

Valley viewsTea plantationsPhotographyShort stop

Pothamedu is one of the convenient viewpoints near Munnar. The viewpoint looks across Munnar's green valleys and plantation-covered hills. Kerala Tourism describes Pothamedu as a viewpoint surrounded by tea, coffee and cardamom plantations, with trekking opportunities nearby, around 6 km from Munnar town.

03

Attukad Waterfall

WaterfallsNaturePhotographyShort walks

Attukad Waterfall is located between Munnar and Pallivasal and is surrounded by rocky hills and greenery. Kerala Tourism places Attukad at about 9 km from Munnar town. The waterfall area is also associated with short walks and trekking. The experience can change considerably with rainfall and season, so the waterfall flow should not be assumed to be the same throughout the year.

04

2nd Mile View Point

Tea-hill sceneryQuick photographsRoute sightseeing

2nd Mile is a scenic stop along the Kochi–Munnar side of the route. It works better as a short viewpoint stop than as a major standalone attraction. Depending on road conditions, traffic and parking, travellers can spend a short time here for photographs before continuing.

05

Hot Air Balloon

Optional activity

Hot-air-balloon experiences are offered in the Munnar area through activity operators. Because balloon rides depend strongly on weather, operating conditions and the individual operator, the activity should be treated as an optional experience, not a guaranteed stop. If you want to include a balloon ride, confirm the operator's current operating slot and activity price before the trip.

Activity charge: separate from taxi fare
06

Elephant Safari / Elephant Ride

Optional activity

Elephant-related experiences are available through individual tourism operators around Munnar. Since activity availability, operating practices, timings and prices can vary between operators, the taxi fare does not include the activity. If you want to add an elephant experience, confirm the specific operator and current availability before booking.

Activity charge: separate from taxi fare
07

Chocolate Factory

FamiliesCouplesShoppingLocal food products

A chocolate-factory visit can be added as a shopping and local-product stop on the Munnar sightseeing route. The experience and products offered depend on the individual establishment, so the exact factory/shop should be confirmed when planning the route.

Entry/activity charges: depend on the establishment.
08

Zip Line

Adventure

Zipline experiences are available around the Munnar/Anachal side. Some adventure parks combine ziplining with other activities such as sky cycling, glass bridges and rope-based experiences. Because this is an operator-run activity, ticket price, operating time, age/height restrictions and weather-related availability should be confirmed before the visit.

Activity charge: separate from taxi fare
09

Kerala Handloom Saree Factory

ShoppingTraditional textilesCultural experience

A handloom or textile visit can be included as a shopping and local-craft stop depending on the route and the specific establishment operating on the day. This is more suitable for travellers interested in Kerala textiles and shopping than for travellers looking only for viewpoints and waterfalls.

Entry/shopping cost: separate.
10

Dream Land Adventure Park

FamiliesGroupsAdventure

Dream Land is an adventure-oriented stop in the Munnar area with multiple recreational activities. If you want to spend proper time inside an adventure park, allow more time than you would for a normal viewpoint or photo stop. Activity tickets are separate from the taxi fare.

Activity charge: separate and subject to the park's current ticket rates.
11

Glass Bridge

Confirm availability before travel

A glass-bridge experience can be considered as an optional adventure stop around Anachal. However, do not plan this attraction without checking current operating status first. The Munnar glass bridge has been subject to government and court proceedings concerning permissions and operation, so availability can change.

Activity charge: separate
12

Sengulam Dam Boating

FamiliesCouplesPhotographyBoating

Sengulam Dam is one of the main activity stops on the Kochi direction. Kerala Hydel Tourism lists boating facilities at Sengulam, including pedal boats, speed boats, pontoon boats and larger boats. This makes Sengulam useful for travellers who want to combine scenic views with a relaxed boating activity.

Boating charge: separate from taxi fare and subject to the current operator/ticket rate.
13

Spice Garden Tour

FamiliesCouplesShoppingLearn about spices

A spice-garden visit gives travellers an opportunity to see Kerala's plantation products and learn about spices such as pepper, cardamom, cinnamon and other plants. Different gardens operate independently, so entry fees, tour duration, opening hours and inclusions are not universal. Choose a spice garden based on your route and available time.

Entry/tour charge: separate.
14

Kathakali Show

CultureFamiliesKerala traditions

Kathakali is an evening cultural experience that can be added when your schedule allows. Current Munnar listings commonly show Kathakali performances around 5:00 PM–6:00 PM, with Kalaripayattu demonstrations often following in the evening. Exact timing and venue should be confirmed before the visit. This is not normally a casual roadside stop, so the day's route should be planned around the show timing.

Ticket: separate.
15

Kalari Show

Cultural experienceEvening activity

Kalaripayattu is a traditional Kerala martial-art performance and is commonly offered after the evening Kathakali performance at some Munnar cultural centres. A current listing for Kalari Kshethra shows a 6:00 PM–7:00 PM Kalarippayattu slot after the Kathakali performance, but show schedules can change.

Ticket: separate.
16

Ayurvedic Massage

RelaxationWellness

An Ayurvedic massage or wellness session can be added through a suitable Munnar wellness centre. This is an appointment-based experience rather than a normal sightseeing stop, so advance confirmation is recommended.

Treatment price: separate and depends on the centre and treatment selected.
17

Kallar Waterfall

NaturePhotographyWaterfall scenery

Kallar is a waterfall/stream-side stop on the Munnar–Adimali side of the Kochi direction. It should not be confused with the Kallar destination near Ponmudi in Thiruvananthapuram district. The Munnar-side Kallar can work as a short scenic stop when conditions permit. Water flow and practical access can vary with rainfall and local conditions.

05 / Planning

Pickup, stops & timing.

Hotel or resort pickup in Munnar

We can arrange pickup from your Munnar hotel, resort, homestay or other suitable pickup location. Share your hotel/resort name, pickup date, preferred pickup time, number of travellers, vehicle requirement, sightseeing places you want to visit and Kochi drop location. We can then plan the route around your actual journey.

Can I add or remove sightseeing stops?

Yes. This is a private sightseeing taxi, so the route can be planned around your requirements.

  • Remove places you are not interested in
  • Add selected viewpoints
  • Spend more time at an activity
  • Choose fewer stops for a relaxed journey
  • Add shopping or food stops
  • Plan around an evening cultural show
  • Continue directly to Kochi after your selected sightseeing

Additional sightseeing charges may apply according to the vehicle and selected points.

How long does Munnar to Kochi sightseeing take?

A direct Munnar → Kochi transfer is much shorter than a sightseeing itinerary. The road distance is roughly 125–130 km, but sightseeing adds time depending on the number of stops, activity duration, traffic, weather and road conditions. For this reason, we do not recommend promising one fixed sightseeing duration for every passenger.

If you have a flight, train or other fixed appointment in Kochi, tell us the required arrival time before selecting sightseeing stops.
