01 / Quick answers

Munnar to Thekkady taxi, in short.

How far is Thekkady?

Roughly 90–100 km, about 3 to 3.5 hours by road.

What is it good for?

Periyar wildlife and boating, spice plantations and a night in Kumily or Thekkady.

Is it a transfer or sightseeing?

This page is the transfer. For the sightseeing version, see the Thekkady route on the sightseeing page.

Can I continue to Alleppey?

Yes. Many guests go Munnar, Thekkady, Alleppey.

02 / The drive

What the road is like.

Hills to plateau

You descend from tea country into spice-growing land near Kumily.

Leave by mid-morning

A mid-morning start reaches Thekkady by lunch and leaves the afternoon for activities.

Rain days

Fog and heavy rain slow the hill sections. Keep spare time.

03 / Thekkady plans

What to do after you arrive.

Timings and availability are set by the park and operators.

Periyar boating

A lake cruise is a common first activity. Book tickets in advance in season.

Spice plantation walk

Short guided walks show cardamom, pepper and clove.

Evening in Kumily

Small markets and spice shops are within easy reach.
