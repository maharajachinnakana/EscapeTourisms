01 / Sightseeing Packages

Six ways to see Munnar from Chinnakanal.

The following packages are based on the sightseeing list provided for Escape Tourisms. Final routes depend on exact attraction locations, travel time, road conditions, visitor access and the activities you choose.

PACKAGE 01

Waterfalls, Gap Road & Adventure Activities

  • Power House / Periyakanal Waterfalls
  • Gap Road View Point
  • Lockhart Tea Garden View Point
  • Pallivasal View Point
  • Chocolate Factory
  • Handloom Saree Factory
  • Zipline
  • Dreamland Fun Park
  • Sengulam Dam
  • Spice Plantation
  • Elephant Ride
  • Hot Air Balloon

This option combines scenic stops with optional commercial attractions. The exact route should be confirmed before booking because some stops may be in different directions. Factory visits and paid activities depend on operator access and availability.

PACKAGE 02

Tea Gardens, Viewpoints & Mattupetty

  • Periyakanal Waterfalls
  • Bison Valley Viewpoint
  • Rock Cave View Point
  • Tea Plantation
  • Tea Factory
  • Idli Mountain View
  • Rose Garden
  • Photo Point
  • Elephant Ride
  • Mattupetty Dam
  • Speed Boat
  • Elephant Arrival Spot
  • Echo Point and boating, where available

This itinerary includes a mixture of viewpoints, plantation-related visits and optional activities. Some attractions are outside the immediate Chinnakanal area, so the stop order and achievable duration must be checked before confirming the trip.

PACKAGE 03

Muttukad Valley & Anayirankal Sightseeing

  • Muttukad Valley View Point
  • Orange Plantation
  • Hanging Bridge View Point
  • Anayirankal Dam View
  • Boating, subject to availability
  • Spice Garden Tour
  • Chathurangapara / Tamil Nadu View Point

Anayirankal is a recognized local attraction surrounded by tea plantations and forest. Boating is offered at the reservoir, but current operating status and charges should be confirmed for the travel date.

Some viewpoint names on this route are confirmed locally when you book.

APPROX. 3 HOURS

Short Sightseeing Trip

Short trip
  • Muttukad Valley View Point
  • Orange Plantation
  • Anayirankal Dam area
  • Spice Plantation visit

The three-hour duration is a proposed package duration, not a guarantee that all stops and activities can be completed within that time. The final itinerary depends on the pickup point, road travel and time spent at each attraction.

PACKAGE 04

Eravikulam National Park & Tea Museum

  • Eravikulam National Park
  • Kannan Devan Tea Museum

This longer excursion combines Eravikulam National Park with the Kannan Devan Tea Museum. The Tea Museum is in Munnar, and Eravikulam National Park has its own entry arrangements and seasonal restrictions. Confirm opening status, ticket arrangements and the practical route before booking. This is a longer excursion, not a short local Chinnakanal circuit.

PACKAGE 05

Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary & Marayoor

  • Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary
  • Lakkam Waterfalls
  • Sandalwood Forest

This is a longer excursion that needs advance route and timing checks. Wildlife sightings are not guaranteed. Sanctuary access, entry charges, permitted activities and waterfall access should be verified before departure.

PACKAGE 06

Periyar / Thekkady Excursion

  • Periyar Lake boating
  • Elephant ride
  • Spice Garden Tour

This is a separate long-distance excursion. The exact pickup, travel time, activity availability and itinerary should be confirmed before booking. For visitors whose main destination is Thekkady, you can also enquire about the dedicated Munnar to Thekkady Sightseeing Taxi service.
