03 / Group size

Innova for 5, 6 and 7 travellers.

Seat count decides the vehicle. Here is how an Innova fits each group in Munnar.

Innova for 5 persons

A relaxed fit for a family of five or friends travelling with day bags. Everyone gets space, and the boot or rear area still takes a few small suitcases. If budget matters more than extra comfort and luggage is light, an Ertiga can also work.

Innova for 6 persons

The sweet spot for the Innova. Six travellers leave one seat free for bags, a jacket or a tired child, which makes a long hill day far easier. Ertiga also seats six but is tighter once luggage comes along.

Innova for 7 persons

Full capacity. Ideal for a sightseeing day where bags are small. If you also carry big suitcases, plan luggage in advance. Above seven people, choose a Traveller or Urbania so nobody is squeezed.

Travelling with 4 or fewer?

04 / XUV & SUV

Looking for an XUV taxi for Munnar sightseeing?

People often search for an XUV for Munnar because they want an SUV-style, high-seating ride that feels sturdy on hill roads.

What we can offer instead

Our listed fleet does not include an XUV. For similar comfort and space we usually suggest:

  • Innova Crysta — spacious SUV-style MUV for up to 7 travellers, from ₹3,000 per route.
  • Innova — the same seating at a lower fare, from ₹2,800 per route.
  • Off-road Jeep — for rough tracks and Kolukkumalai / Chathurangapara-style experiences, handled on our jeep safari page.

If an XUV is a must for your trip, send us your date and group size. We will tell you honestly whether we can arrange it.
