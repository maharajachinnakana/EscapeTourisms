01 / Quick answers

Munnar to Varkala, in short.

How far is Varkala?

About 290 km, so this is a full travel day of roughly 7 to 8.5 hours.

Is it one-way or round trip?

Most guests treat it as a one-way move from hills to the beach.

When should we leave?

Early morning, so you reach the Varkala cliff before sunset.

What is Varkala known for?

Papanasam beach, the cliff walk with cafes and a quiet resort stay.

02 / Why Varkala

Why couples pick Varkala after Munnar.

Cliff-side beach

Sea cliffs, sunset walks and cafes, a different mood from the hill stations.

Slow stays

Resorts and homestays along the cliff suit a restful end to the trip.

Papanasam

A beach with a religious bathing tradition.

03 / Plan the day

Travel-day checklist.

  • Leave Munnar early; keep one lunch break
  • Share resort name and map pin
  • Ask for the vehicle that fits luggage
  • Plan to arrive before sunset
