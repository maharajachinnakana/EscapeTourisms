01 / Quick answers

Coimbatore airport to Munnar, in short.

How far is it?

About 150–155 km by road, 4 to 5 hours depending on traffic and weather.

Which route?

Pollachi, then Udumalpet and the Marayoor side. An alternate Udumalpet–Palladam road takes about an hour more.

Why choose this airport?

Good flight links from Tamil Nadu and Bengaluru, and a scenic road past Chinnar.

Best months?

September to May. Monsoon can bring landslide delays.

02 / Route

What you pass.

Aliyar and Pollachi

Aliyar Dam and Monkey Falls are short stops near Pollachi.

Chinnar side

The road passes near Chinnar wildlife sanctuary and Marayoor.

Final climb

The last stretch climbs into Munnar tea country.
