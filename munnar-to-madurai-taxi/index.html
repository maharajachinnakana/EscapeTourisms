01 / Quick answers

Munnar to Madurai, in short.

How far is Madurai from Munnar?

About 155 km by road.

How long does the drive take?

Around 4.5 hours on the NH85 route via Bodinayakanur and Theni, or about 5 hours on the NH183 route via Kumily and Kambam.

Can I go to the airport or railway station?

Yes. Madurai airport, junction and hotel drops are all possible.

Is it a good time to visit?

September to March is generally the most comfortable window; monsoon brings the greenest views.

02 / Routes

Two ways into Madurai.

NH85 via Bodinayakanur and Theni

About 4.5 hours. The common choice for a direct transfer.

NH183 via Kumily and Kambam

About 5 hours. Pass near Thekkady and add a Periyar stop if time allows.

03 / In Madurai

Meenakshi Amman temple and the old city.

Temple visiting hours and dress rules change; check the day before.

Temple visit

Arrive early to avoid crowds; carry modest clothing and keep phones and bags minimal.

Food

Madurai is known for its street food and meals; plan a food stop in the old city.

Evening

Parking near the temple is limited; the driver can drop you at the nearest access point.

04 / Onward travel

After Madurai.

Madurai airport

Direct drops to the airport are common for flights onward.

Railway junction

Madurai Junction connects to most of Tamil Nadu.

Rameswaram and Kanyakumari

Long drives; discuss a multi-day plan with us.
