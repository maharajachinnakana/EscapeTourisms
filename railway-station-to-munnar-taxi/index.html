01 / Quick answers

Railway station to Munnar, in short.

Does Munnar have a railway station?

No. Travellers arrive by train at Aluva or Ernakulam and continue to Munnar by road.

Which station is best?

Aluva is usually the closest start for the Munnar road. Ernakulam Junction and Ernakulam Town work when your train ends there.

How long is the drive?

Roughly 3.5 to 4.5 hours, depending on the station, traffic, stops and weather.

How much does the taxi cost?

From ₹3,500 when a one-way taxi is available, or ₹4,500 when it is not, per vehicle.

05 / Train timing

Arrival time decides your plan.

Early morning (4–8 AM)

Best case. You reach Munnar by late morning with daylight for stops.

Daytime

Comfortable. Add one waterfall stop if the hill road is dry.

Evening or night

Hill roads are slower in the dark. Many travellers stay near Kochi and start in the morning. Ask us first.

Delayed trains

Tell us your PNR and train number. We follow the running status and adjust the pickup.
