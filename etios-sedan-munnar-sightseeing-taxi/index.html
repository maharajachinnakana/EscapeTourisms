02 / Quick answers

Sedan sightseeing in Munnar, in short.

How much is an Etios for Munnar sightseeing?

Etios routes start from ₹2,300 per vehicle; Swift Dzire from ₹2,000. Local full-day Munnar sightseeing in a sedan starts from ₹2,000.

How many people fit in a sedan?

Up to 4 passengers. For 5 or more, an Ertiga or Innova is more comfortable.

Why choose a sedan?

It is the lowest-fare private vehicle, easy to park and ideal for solo travellers, couples and small families.

Can I choose my own stops?

Yes. Use the Customise trip button on any route to add or remove points and send them on WhatsApp.

03 / Group size

Sedan for 1, 2, 3 and 4 persons.

Seat count and luggage decide whether a sedan is right. Here is how it works for each group.

1 person

A private sedan gives a solo traveller flexibility and safety on hill roads. The fare is per vehicle, so it is best when you value privacy and your own schedule.

2 persons

The classic couple option: private, quiet and easy to slow down for photographs at tea gardens and viewpoints.

3 persons

Plenty of room for three with day bags. A good balance of comfort and cost for friends or a small family.

4 persons

Full capacity. Works well for a sightseeing day with small bags. Heavy luggage or a fifth traveller means moving up to an Ertiga or Innova.

04 / Why sedan

Why a sedan works for Munnar.

Lowest private fare

Swift Dzire and Etios carry the lowest starting rates in our fleet, so your sightseeing budget goes further.

Easy at crowded stops

A sedan is simpler to park at busy viewpoints and narrow town streets.

Efficient for small groups

No need to pay for extra seats you will not use when you travel as 1 to 4 people.

Know its limits

Not suited to five or more travellers, bulky luggage or rough off-road tracks. For those, see Innova, Ertiga or our Jeep safari.
