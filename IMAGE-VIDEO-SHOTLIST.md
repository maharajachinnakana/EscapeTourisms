# Escape Tourisms — Image & Video Shot List

This is a page-by-page map of every image/video slot in the new build, and what
to shoot or source for each. Filenames match what the HTML already expects —
just drop your final files into the matching `assets/images/...` folder with
these exact names (or tell me the new names and I'll update the HTML).

Formats: use `.webp` for photos (smaller, fast-loading), `.mp4` for video.
Target size: under 300KB per photo, under 4MB per short video clip.

## General notes

- Shoot photos in **landscape orientation**, 16:9 or wider — all image slots
  on the site are wide cards, portrait shots get cropped badly.
- For every vehicle (Etios, Ertiga, Innova Crysta, offroad Jeep, Swift Dzire,
  Traveller Urbania) get at least 1 clean exterior shot — parked, no people,
  good light. These aren't wired into the pricing cards yet but strongly
  recommended for a future "Our Fleet" section.
- Once you send images, tell me which slot each one is for (using the table
  labels above) and I'll wire them into the HTML directly.
