02 / The route

What this package covers.

Four stops, little driving and no early start. It suits families with children, grandparents and anyone who wants to see Munnar without a dawn alarm.

Eravikulam can close on some days and during parts of the year, so we check before the trip and suggest a swap if it is shut.
